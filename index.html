01 — About the designer

Cloth is a
slow language.

JOGI. is a small textile and surface design studio, founded in 2026 on a simple belief: a pattern should feel as if a hand has passed over it.

Every collection begins away from the screen — with ink, gouache and a block of paper. Motifs are drawn, cut and redrawn until they hold together on their own, then built into repeats that run quietly across cloth, paper and walls.

The studio works with fashion labels, interior brands and independent makers, designing prints that respect the material they will live on: the slub of linen, the weight of cotton, the grain of a wall.

03 — Process

From sketch to cloth

  1. 01

    Sketch

    Loose studies in ink, pencil and gouache. Plants, architecture, old cloth — anything with a rhythm worth keeping.

  2. 02

    Motif

    The strongest marks are isolated, refined and redrawn until each motif can stand alone, at any scale.

  3. 03

    Repeat

    Motifs are engineered into seamless repeats — half-drop, brick or mirror — so the pattern flows without a visible join.

  4. 04

    Colourway

    Palettes are mixed from natural dye references — indigo, madder, walnut — and tested as several colourways per design.

  5. 05

    Print / Weave

    Final artwork is prepared for screen, block or digital print, or translated into a weave draft, with production-ready files.
